A PROMISE OF HOPE

Will Jesus Christ Come Again?

The Second Coming of Jesus Christ is an important subject in Christian faith. In John 14:3, Jesus promised His disciples that He would come again and receive them to Himself.

This promise gives Christians hope and encourages believers to remain faithful, seek God's guidance, and live with love, humility, and spiritual readiness.

But what does the Bible reveal about His coming? How should we understand biblical prophecy? These are questions worth exploring carefully through the words of Scripture.

"I will come again, and receive you unto myself." JOHN 14:3 — KJV (EXCERPT)
EXPLORE THE SCRIPTURES

Understanding the Lord's Return

Discover key biblical passages and reflect on what they teach about Christ's coming, the last days, and the importance of being spiritually prepared.

01 — THE PROMISE

The Promise of His Coming

The Bible presents the return of Jesus as a promise that gives hope to His followers. Acts 1:11 records the message that Jesus, who was taken up into heaven, will come again.

This promise invites us to consider our relationship with God and how we live out our faith each day.

READ: JOHN 14:1–3 · ACTS 1:9–11
02 — BIBLICAL PROPHECY

The Signs of the Last Days

In Matthew 24, Jesus spoke about wars, reports of wars, famines, earthquakes, and other troubling events. He also warned His disciples not to be alarmed and encouraged them to remain watchful.

Studying these passages requires careful reading and prayerful reflection. We should not assume that every world event proves a specific prophetic timeline.

READ: MATTHEW 24:4–14
03 — SPIRITUAL READINESS

Are We Ready for His Coming?

Jesus repeatedly encouraged His followers to watch and be ready. Spiritual readiness is not simply knowing about prophecy; it also involves faithfulness, love, prayer, and obedience to God's teachings.

His words invite us to examine our hearts and consider how we can live with greater sincerity and responsibility.

READ: MATTHEW 24:42–44
04 — HOPE AND HOLINESS

Living in Hope and Faith

Second Peter 3 encourages believers to live holy and godly lives while looking forward to God's promises. Christian hope is meant to shape how we treat others and how we live today.

Instead of living in fear, we can use this hope as an encouragement to pursue truth, peace, compassion, and a closer relationship with God.

READ: 2 PETER 3:8–14
A WORD TO REMEMBER
"Watch therefore: for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come."
MATTHEW 24:42 — KJV

The Bible does not give us permission to set a date for Christ's return. Instead, Jesus calls His followers to watchfulness and faithful living.

PERSONAL REFLECTION

Questions Worth Exploring Together

Studying the Bible is an opportunity to ask sincere questions, examine Scripture, and grow in understanding.

01. What does the Bible say about Christ's return?

Explore John 14:1–3 and Acts 1:9–11. What promises and assurances do these passages offer?

02. How should we understand the signs of the last days?

Read Matthew 24 in context. What warnings and instructions did Jesus give His disciples?

03. What does spiritual readiness mean?

Reflect on Matthew 24:42–44. How can faith, prayer, love, and obedience shape our daily lives?

04. How can we seek a deeper understanding of God's Word?

Consider how careful Bible reading, prayer, discussion, and a willingness to learn can help us grow in faith.
